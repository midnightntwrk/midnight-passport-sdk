# E02.03 Voice and copy guidelines

**Epic:** E02 · Passport app: Brand and identity · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 2  
**Epic issue:** [#45](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/45)  
**Packages:** app  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** App identity and personality

## Description

How the app looks, feels, and talks to users, so it reads as an identity product rather than a wallet.

Sets the voice for consent copy, such as the prover disclosure and grant scopes, so it reads plainly to people who have never used a wallet.

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E02.01 Brand tokens: color, type, iconography  
**Unlocks:** no other story.

## Acceptance criteria

- The guidelines include worked examples for the prover disclosure and a grant scope.
- No screen copy uses wallet jargon such as seed phrase or gas.
