# E06.03 Returning-user account resume

**Epic:** E06 · Passport app: onboarding · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 10  
**Epic issue:** [#49](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/49)  
**Packages:** The Passport app. From the SDK: owner  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Return to the account

## Description

On a later visit the app opens the user's existing account and never shows sign-up again. On the same device this needs no passkey prompt just to open the account: the local record and the chain are enough, and the passkey is asked for when the user does something. On a wiped or new device it depends on a design that is not chosen yet.

Cannot start yet. Same gate as E06.01 (onboarding flow): E02.01 (app design) and an app repository.

In scope: App start: call the lookup of E04.13 (existing-account lookup) and route to sign-up, to deploy progress (an unfinished deploy), or to the account. A "sign in with your passkey" path for a device with no local record, built on part 2 of E04.13 once that is decided. States for: indexer unreachable (retry, never "no account"), account on an unsupported contract build, passkey present but no account found. A guard so a returning user cannot start a second account by accident.

Out of scope: the lookup logic (E04.13). Restoring private data on a new device, which is E17.05 (WPP restore) and E20 (Passport app: backup and devices).

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E04.13 Existing-account lookup; E06.01 Onboarding flow: passkey creation  
**Unlocks:** nothing on the board.

## Acceptance criteria

- On the same browser profile, a returning user lands on their account with 0 passkey prompts and no sign-up screen (UI test).
- A user who left during the deploy lands on the progress screen at the right step.
- With the indexer down, the app shows a retry state and does not offer sign-up.
- "Create a new account" is not reachable while a local record exists, except behind an explicit confirmation.
- Once part 2 of E04.13 (existing-account lookup) lands: on a wiped profile, one passkey prompt finds the account.
