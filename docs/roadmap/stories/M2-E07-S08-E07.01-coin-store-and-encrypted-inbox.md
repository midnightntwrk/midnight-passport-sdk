# E07.01 Coin store and encrypted inbox

**Epic:** E07 · Payments · **Milestone:** 2 Account management and core actions · **Step:** 8

## Description

Track the account's coins; incoming payments arrive encrypted in the account's inbox.

The coin store tracks the account's coins. Incoming payments are sealed to the recipient key at the time of the seal (rule 7) and opened from the inbox.

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E04.12 Multi-wave ACC deploy planner  
**Unlocks:** E07.02 Transfers via deposit calls; E08.02 Activity feed (indexer and inbox); E25.03 ETH and BTC deposit flow

## Acceptance criteria

- Incoming payments are opened from the inbox and appear in the coin store.
- Each payment is sealed to the recipient key current at the time of the seal (rule 7).
