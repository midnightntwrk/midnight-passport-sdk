**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Send and receive

## Description

The screens where the owner pays someone: an ordinary wallet address, or another Passport. Each payment is one transaction, except that a shielded send with change asks for the passkey twice, and an unshielded payment to another Passport has two steps.

Board title: Transfer and deposit screens. Split. This story keeps the code and the sending screens. Receiving is the new story E08.05 (receive screen and deposit page).

Cannot start yet. It needs the component library from E02.01 (app design) and a repository for the Passport app. Neither exists.

The SDK stories behind it are not all ready. Sending to a wallet address needs E07.03 (send to a wallet address). Paying another Passport needs E07.02 (pay another Passport, shielded), which is to discuss, and by name it is blocked from outside with E04.18 (name resolution).

In scope: Pick an asset from the holdings of E07.05 (holdings and activity reads). The recipient: a name once names exist, or a wallet address that is pasted or scanned. Parse errors are shown inline. An account contract address is refused with an explanation (rule 3, deposit, not address). The amount. For shielded funds the largest possible amount is the largest single coin, and the screen says so. A review step: who, what, the fee shown as covered, and how many times the passkey will be asked. A payment between two Passports says that it links the two accounts in public. Progress from the SDK's events, and the result. Work in progress comes from the pending payments read of E07.03, here and on every other screen. Opening the app with an unfinished payment shows it and lets the SDK finish it. Only for the unshielded payment of E07.06 (pay another Passport, unshielded), and only if that story is built: two steps shown as such, and the outcome "returned to your account".

Out of scope: The payment flows, which are E07.02, E07.03 and E07.06. Receiving, which is E08.05. Payments asked for by a dApp, which is E15.05 (Pay with Passport).

## Acceptance criteria

- UI tests against stubbed SDK flows cover every state. They run before the SDK stories exist.
- Once E07.03 (send to a wallet address) exists: on a local node, a send to a wallet address completes from the screens, with the second prompt explained when there is change.
- Once E07.02 (pay another Passport, shielded) exists: a shielded payment to a second Passport completes from the screens.
- Closing the tab while a send is being proved and opening the app again shows the payment as pending and then as done or failed.
- A test scans the rendered text of every send screen: the account's contract address never appears as a payment address.
