# E04.01 Sponsor policy and typed refusals

**Epic:** E04 · Account deployment · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 1  
**Epic issue:** [#47](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/47)  
**Packages:** core, docs/  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Sponsor policy

## Description

Write down what the sponsor pays for and what it refuses, and give the SDK one typed error for each refusal with a rule for what to do next. The board text says the policy is "agreed with the proving & settlement service". No such service exists. The sponsor is a Capacity Exchange Service that the provider runs for Preview, so the policy is ours to write and the product owner's to approve.

In scope: A policy doc: which transactions are paid (account deploys, maintenance updates that install account contract verifier keys, calls to account contract circuits), a fee cap per transaction type, and a limit per caller. The typed refusal errors in `core`, and a table that maps each sponsor answer and each node refusal to one of three actions: retry the same request, rebuild and pay again, or stop. The rule that the client sends proven transactions only, and submits within the sponsor's time limit.

Out of scope: enforcing the policy on the server. That is work on the sponsor service, outside this repo, and it has to be tracked somewhere (see open questions). The client code that talks to the sponsor, which is E04.04 (broadcast adapter).

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** nothing. It can start on day one.  
**Unlocks:** E04.04 adapter-broadcast: sponsor payment, submit, follow, resubmit; E04.09 Starting balance

## Acceptance criteria

- The policy doc is merged and names: what is paid, the fee cap per transaction type, the per-caller limit, and who can change them.
- `core` exports one error per refusal: busy, over the cap, not an allowed transaction, caller limit reached, sponsor unavailable.
- The action table covers the sponsor's HTTP answers and the node refusals seen in tests (expired intent, DUST proof refused, DUST double spend, fee too low, over the block limit), each with a test.
- No refusal error contains the transaction bytes or a key.
- The doc says that grant-key calls from dApps and agents are paid, or says who pays them.

## Open questions

- Per-caller limit: a sign-up token, a limit per IP, or a budget per account? Product decision for the product owner.
- Where is the server-side work tracked? It is not in this repo.
- Can a call that the node refuses for a low fee be retried at a higher fee through the sponsor?
- Does the sponsor pay for adding a signing arm later (a large maintenance update)?
