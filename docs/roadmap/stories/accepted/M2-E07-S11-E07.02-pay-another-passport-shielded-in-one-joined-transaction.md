# E07.02 Pay another Passport: shielded, in one joined transaction

**Epic:** E07 · Payments · **Milestone:** 2 Account management and core actions · **Step:** 11  
**Epic issue:** [#50](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/50)  
**Packages:** core, owner  
**Readiness:** to discuss implementation · **Sheet title:** Send and receive

## Description

Pay another Passport from a Passport, in shielded funds. An account is a contract, so value enters it only through its deposit circuits (rule 3, deposit, not address). The SDK documents define this payment as one transaction: `pay` joins the payer's `withdraw_shielded_to_contract` with the recipient's `deposit_shielded`, and seals a 192-byte inbox entry to the recipient's encryption key. There is no wallet in between and no state between two steps. The transaction links the two accounts in public; amount, color and nonce stay hidden.

Board title: Transfers via deposit calls. Split in three. This story keeps the code and the shielded payment to another Passport. The withdrawal to a wallet address is the new story E07.03 (send to a wallet address); the unshielded payment is the new story E07.06 (pay another Passport, unshielded).

To discuss before it starts. The joined transaction cannot be built with stock midnight-js 5.0.0, which refuses a shielded coin sent to a contract unless the same call claims it. It needs a composer, and the composer is the joiner of E15.01 (intent joiner), a story that sits in a later milestone. See the open questions.

Paying by name is blocked from outside with E04.18 (name resolution), which waits on the name service. Paying an account by an identifier is not.

In scope: `core`: `pay` to an account. It builds the payer's call to `withdraw_shielded_to_contract` with the call builder of E07.03, builds the recipient's `deposit_shielded` with the builder of E07.01 (inbox, coin store and deposits), and joins the two with the joiner of E15.01. This story does not write a joiner. The inbox entry for the recipient is sealed to the recipient's encryption key, read from the chain at seal time and again for each retry (rule 7). The recipient: by name through E04.18; until that exists, by an account identifier the caller passes, behind the same interface. The payer's change coin, its inbox entry and a stop in the middle are handled by the journal of E07.03. This story adds no state of its own. A typed result for each way it can end: paid, refused before sending, not spent with the fee charged. No API returns an account's contract address formatted as a payment address. References: test 8 of `contract/src/tests/custody-payments.ts` in the passport repo, which composes this transaction.

Out of scope: The joiner itself, which is E15.01. Unshielded payments, which are E07.06. Payments asked for by a dApp, which is E15.05 (Pay with Passport). A deposit from an ordinary wallet, which is the builder of E07.01 and the page of E08.05 (receive screen and deposit page).

## Acceptance criteria

- On a local node, with two p256 accounts and a test token: a shielded payment from A is one transaction. B's inbox gains one entry, B's client receives the coin through E07.01 (inbox, coin store and deposits), and B then spends it with E07.03 (send to a wallet address).
- A's change coin is in A's store, and A's inbox has its entry.
- Unit test: when the read of the recipient key returns one key and then another on retry, the second attempt is sealed to the second key.
- The process is killed after the transaction is sent. After a restart the journal reconciles and A's store matches the chain.
- A payment to an address that is not an account contract is refused with a typed error before any transaction.
- The by-name path is tested with a stub resolver until E04.18 (name resolution) lands.
- The tests assert the passkey prompts when no re-prompt occurs: one for the payment, and one more for the change entry when there is change, because the owner form of `withdraw_shielded_to_contract` does not take one either. They record transactions and seconds per payment.

## Open questions

- The composer. On an earlier stack the joined transaction landed once, built by hand through a client with one midnight-js check removed. On the current stack stock midnight-js cannot build the payer's call at all. Does the joiner of E15.01 (intent joiner) carry its own composer, or does this wait for an upstream change? To settle before this story starts.
- E15.01 is in a later milestone than this story. Either the joiner moves earlier, or this story moves later. Which?
- The custody spec (MIP-0012, section 6.6) says payments between two custody contracts must go as one hop, where the recipient deposits into its own account. The SDK documents choose the joined form and accept the public link. Is that the decision for V1?
- Not measured: this payment with passkey accounts, on the current stack, in any form.
- Without names, what does a payer type or scan to name the payee? An account identifier is not a payment address (rule 3), but something has to identify the account. Product decision.
- The SDK requirements say no circuit moves NIGHT between two accounts. Which assets does V1 pay between Passports?
