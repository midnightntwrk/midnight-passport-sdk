# E05.03 ACC artifact loader (module, decoder, manifest)

**Epic:** E05 · Contract binding · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 4  
**Epic issue:** [#48](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/48)  
**Packages:** contract  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Load the contract artifact

## Description

Load the compiled contract module, its ledger decoder, and its manifest from the published artifact.

The SDK never compiles or owns the ACC. It consumes the published artifact and checks it against the manifest before use.

**Blocked by:** E03.02 Monorepo scaffold for mn-passport-* packages; E05.01 ACC artifact build and publication (ADR)  
**Unlocks:** E04.11 Multi-wave ACC deploy planner; E05.04 Binding version registry; E05.05 dApp artifact loader with integrity checks; E21.01 Intent joiner: dApp intent + ACC payment

## Acceptance criteria

- `contract` loads the module, ledger decoder, and manifest, and checks them against the manifest.
- A tampered artifact is rejected.
