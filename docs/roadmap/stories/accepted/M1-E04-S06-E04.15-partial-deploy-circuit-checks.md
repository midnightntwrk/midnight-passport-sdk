# E04.15 Partial-deploy circuit checks

**Epic:** E04 · Account deployment · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 6  
**Epic issue:** [#47](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/47)  
**Packages:** contract, core  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Partly deployed account

## Description

The SDK works with an account that does not have every circuit of the compiled contract on chain, and each call requires only the circuits it uses.

On the board this was a late convenience (use the account before the last wave lands). It is now required from the first account: accounts are born with the p256 arm only (20 of 52 circuits) and gain the JubJub grant-spend circuits by a later update, and stock midnight-js refuses to bind to such a contract.

In scope: A binding in `contract` that verifies only the circuits a caller names, against the verifier keys on chain, and restricts calls to those circuits. A typed error when a call needs a circuit that is not deployed, naming the circuit and the arm. A read: which arms and circuits this account has. While the maintenance authority is live, re-check the verifier key of a circuit before each call that uses it.

Out of scope: deploying the missing circuits, which is E04.22 (add circuits to a live account). The loader itself, which is E05.03 (ACC artifact loader).

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E05.03 ACC artifact loader (module, decoder, manifest). Test accounts come from the provider's deploy scripts until the SDK can deploy.  
**Unlocks:** E04.12 Account deploy in waves: plan, send, resume, activate; E04.22 Add circuits to a live account: grant-spend circuits and later arms

## Acceptance criteria

- On a local node, activation and an owner call succeed on an account with only wave 1 deployed, and on a one-arm account.
- A call to a circuit of an arm that is not deployed fails before any prompt or proof, with the typed error.
- A circuit whose on-chain verifier key differs from the manifest is refused with a typed error (test with a tampered key).
- The "which arms" read returns the right answer for a wave-1-only, a one-arm and a full account.
- No call path uses midnight-js's full-contract check.
