# E04.11 p256 arm: non-PRF passkeys as owner keys

**Epic:** E04 · Account deployment · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 6  
**Epic issue:** [#47](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/47)  
**Packages:** core, adapter-browser  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Passkey on p256

## Description

Support passkeys that sign on the p256 curve, which most phones and laptops use.

The p256 arm is verified by the ACC directly, and the key never leaves the authenticator. It covers authenticators without PRF.

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E04.02 KeyProvider across k256, p256, and JubJub  
**Unlocks:** no other story.

## Acceptance criteria

- A passkey without PRF becomes an owner key on the p256 arm.
- The key never leaves the authenticator.
