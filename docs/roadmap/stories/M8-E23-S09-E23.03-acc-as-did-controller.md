**Epic:** E23 · Identity and credentials · **Milestone:** 8 Use Cases · **Step:** 9

**Packages:** contract, adapter-did  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** DID controlled by the account

## Description

Control of the DID moves from the app to the account contract.

The ACC becomes the DID's controller, so device changes and recovery keep control of it.
