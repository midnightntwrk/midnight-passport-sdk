# E02.01 App design: UX flow, pages, and design foundations

**Epic:** E02 · Passport app: brand and identity · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 1  
**Epic issue:** [#45](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/45)  
**Packages:** The Passport app. No SDK package  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** App design

## Description

Design the Passport app as a whole before its screens are built: the flows a user goes through, the pages those flows need, and the shared foundations every page uses. Every app story in E06 (Passport app: onboarding) and E08 (Passport app: the account) builds one part of this design, so it has to exist first. The product is Midnight-branded and reads as a personal account, not a crypto wallet.

This story replaces the three earlier drafts of this epic (brand tokens, component library, and voice and copy). They are one piece of design work.

In scope: The UX flows, end to end: onboarding (create a passkey, deploy, activate, and an optional name), returning on the same device, the account home, sending to a wallet address and to another Passport, receiving, activity, and approving a dApp's grant request after a redirect, including a new user who arrives with a request. Later flows (devices, backup, and recovery) are sketched at the level of where they enter the app. The page map: every page, how the user gets there, and what it shows. A wireframe for each page in the milestone 1 and 2 flows. The passkey and consent moments: one pattern for each passkey prompt (creating the account, activating it, and each owner call such as a grant) and for each consent (the prover disclosure, rule 2, and a grant's scope). The states every page needs: loading and progress, empty, stale data, errors with the next step, and an unsupported device. The design foundations: the color palette for light and dark themes, type, iconography, the core components, and the voice and copy rules (plain words, no "seed phrase" or "gas").

Out of scope: building the pages, which the app stories in E06 and E08 do. The app repository and its stack. The flows of later milestones in detail.

## Acceptance criteria

- The flows above are drawn end to end, each with its passkey prompt count stated.
- The page map lists every page of the milestone 1 and 2 flows, and each has a wireframe.
- One pattern exists for every passkey prompt and every consent, with worked copy for the prover disclosure and a grant scope.
- Loading, progress, empty, stale, error, and unsupported-device states are defined.
- The foundations ship as design tokens and components the app can import, in light and dark themes, with contrast and focus checked.
- The product owners sign off the flows and the page map.
