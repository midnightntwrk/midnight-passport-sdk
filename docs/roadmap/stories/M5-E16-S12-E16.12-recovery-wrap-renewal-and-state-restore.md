# E16.12 Recovery wrap renewal and state restore

**Epic:** E16 · Guardian recovery · **Milestone:** 5 Account recovery · **Step:** 12  
**Epic issue:** [#59](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/59)  
**Packages:** owner, core  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Restore private history

## Description

Bring back the private records after recovery, not just the account.

The recovery wrap is renewed after each viewing-key rotation, so a recovered account gets back its private history, not only its keys.

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E13.06 WPP restore on a new device; E16.09 Recovery finalize after the delay  
**Unlocks:** E17.01 Viewing-key rotation on revocation; E17.02 Read-grant viewing-key lifecycle (rule 13)

## Acceptance criteria

- A recovered account gets back its private history; the wrap is renewed after each viewing-key rotation.
