# E04.09 Starting balance

**Epic:** E04 · Account deployment · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 9  
**Epic issue:** [#47](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/47)  
**Packages:** owner, adapter-broadcast  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Start with nothing

## Description

After the account is activated, the sponsor adds a small starting balance, so a new user has something to act with and never needs DUST of their own (rule 14, no user-held DUST).

Board title: Sponsored deploy and starting balance. The story was split. "The sponsor pays the deploy" is now in E04.04 (broadcast adapter) and E04.12 (wave deploy). This story keeps only the starting balance.

Blocked from outside, not for sprint one. A plain NIGHT deposit to an account is refused by the network today (servicedesk#117, midnight-ledger#761).

In scope: A written decision on the token and the amount, and on who funds it. One call in `owner` that asks for the starting balance once per account, after activation. A guard against a second request for the same account, on the sponsor's side and in the SDK. A read of the balance for the UI.

Out of scope: paying the deploy and call fees, which is E04.04 (broadcast adapter) and E04.12 (wave deploy). Transfers and deposits in general, which are E07 (payments).

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E04.12 Account deploy in waves: plan, send, resume, activate; E04.04 adapter-broadcast: sponsor payment, submit, follow, resubmit; E04.01 Sponsor policy and typed refusals. External: servicedesk#117 and midnight-ledger#761, or the workaround of joining the deposit to another call.  
**Unlocks:** E06.02 Deploy progress and balance UI. Only the balance part of that story.

## Acceptance criteria

- The decision is recorded in the sponsor policy doc from E04.01 (sponsor policy): token, amount, limit per account.
- On Preview, a new account shows the starting balance after activation, with no funds in the user's wallet at any point.
- A second request for the same account is refused with a typed error.
- If the deposit fails, the account stays usable and the SDK reports "balance pending" with a typed reason.
