# E04.22 Add circuits to a live account: grant-spend circuits and later arms

**Epic:** E04 · Account deployment · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 9  
**Epic issue:** [#47](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/47)  
**Packages:** owner, contract  
**Readiness:** to discuss implementation · **Sheet title:** none (new story)

## Description

Install more circuits on an account that already exists, through maintenance updates. First the JubJub grant-spend circuits, which the beta needs before a dApp can call under its grant. Later a whole second arm: k256 for a backup key or a wallet, JubJub for agents. Nothing on the board does this.

Proposed by the provider. This story is not on the board. It follows from the agreement that accounts are born with the p256 arm only and gain other circuits later.

In the beta. The first deploy is strictly p256-only. A dApp's grant key is a software JubJub key, so its calls need no passkey prompt, and the circuits that verify that key reach the account through this story.

In scope, first slice (beta). Plan the update for a named circuit set with the planner from E04.12 (wave deploy); the set is read from the manifest. Sign it with the maintenance authority, in whatever form E04.20 (account secrets spec) chooses: a held key, or a signature made at birth. Send, follow and resume like a deploy wave; the sponsor pays. A read: which circuit sets this account has, and can it still add one. Run it as part of account creation, so a new account is ready for grants without a second visit. The first grant can be issued before it lands; the dApp's first call under the grant cannot.

In scope, second slice (after the beta): a whole k256 or JubJub arm, and retiring the authority after the last addition.

Not yet shown anywhere: an addition of circuits to an account that is already in use, and a grant from a p256 owner spent with a JubJub key on such an account. Until now, maintenance updates were only sent seconds after the deploy.

Out of scope: enrolling a key on a new arm, which is E04.17 (WaaS backup owner key) and E19.03 (k256 wallet as an owner key). Issuing and spending grants, which are E10.03 (`issue_grant` call) and E15.03 (grant-key call pipeline).

**Unlocks:** E04.21 Example page: create an account and approve a grant; E15.03 Grant-key call pipeline: sign, prove, broadcast; E04.17 WaaS key as isolated backup owner key; E19.03 k256 wallet as an owner key. For E04.21, its grant slice. Also agent grant keys.

## Acceptance criteria

- On a local node, a p256-only account gains the JubJub grant-spend circuits; a grant issued by the p256 owner is then spent with a JubJub grant key.
- The same on Preview, with the sponsor paying.
- A spend attempted before the circuits are installed fails with the typed "circuit not deployed" error of E04.15 (partial-deploy binding), before any proof.
- An interrupted update resumes without paying twice.
- The test records transactions, time and DUST for the update.
- Second slice: a p256-only account gains the k256 arm and a k256 key makes an owner call; after retirement the "can add" read is false and an attempt fails before anything is sent.

## Open questions

- Who holds the authority key between birth and this update? If the update is signed at birth and only the signature is kept, no key has to live anywhere. Needs the contract owner, before E04.12 (wave deploy) starts.
- The exact grant-spend circuit set. Three JubJub circuits by our reading (unshielded, shielded, shielded to a contract).
- Does the user confirm with the passkey? The chain does not require it: the authority alone can install circuits.
- When is the authority retired, and who decides? While it is live it can replace any verifier key.
- Cost: a few DUST for three small keys; about 20 to 25 DUST for a whole arm (both inferred from a per-byte cost, not measured).
