# E05.05 dApp artifact loader with integrity checks

**Epic:** E05 · Contract binding · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 5  
**Epic issue:** [#48](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/48)  
**Packages:** contract  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** dApp artifact loader

## Description

Load a dApp's contract artifacts and check their integrity before the SDK uses them.

Artifacts are fetched by content hash through the registry seam. The verifier key is made again from the ZKIR and the SRS and compared with the one on chain.

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E05.03 ACC artifact loader (module, decoder, manifest)  
**Unlocks:** no other story.

## Acceptance criteria

- dApp artifacts are fetched by content hash.
- An artifact whose verifier key does not match the chain is rejected.
