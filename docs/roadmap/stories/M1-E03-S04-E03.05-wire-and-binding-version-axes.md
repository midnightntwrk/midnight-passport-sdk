# E03.05 Wire and binding version axes

**Epic:** E03 · Workspace, seams, and guardrails · **Milestone:** 1 Foundation · **Step:** 4

**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** Version axes

## Description

Track two independent versions: the wire protocol version and the version of the contract binding against the deployed account contract.

The wire axis is PROTOCOL_VERSION in protocol. The binding axis is contract against the deployed ACC (rule 5). The two never share one number, and docs/compatibility.md holds the matrix.
