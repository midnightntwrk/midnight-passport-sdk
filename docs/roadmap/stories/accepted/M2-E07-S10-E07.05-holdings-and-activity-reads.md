# E07.05 Holdings and activity reads

**Epic:** E07 · Payments · **Milestone:** 2 Account management and core actions · **Step:** 10  
**Epic issue:** [#50](https://github.com/midnightntwrk/midnight-passport-sdk/issues/50)  
**Packages:** core  
**Readiness:** ready · **Sheet title:** none (new story)

## Description

The account screens need two reads: what the account holds, per color, and what happened on it. The board gives the activity feed to the app, "built from the indexer seam and the inbox". That would put chain decoding in the app. This story puts both reads in `core`, so the screens of E08 (Passport app: the account) only draw.

Proposed by the provider. This story is not on the board.

In scope: Holdings per color, in one read with the `subscribe` and `current` shape of E03.10 (Effect 4 inside the SDK), following the account's state stream. The unshielded amount comes from the account's public state. The shielded amount comes from the coin store of E07.01 (inbox, coin store and deposits): held coins, with candidates not yet checked shown apart. This story owns the balance read for the UI. E04.09 (starting balance) lists one as well; it should use this one. Activity: ordered records decoded from the account's contract actions. Kinds: deploy and updates, deposit in, send out, owner key change, grant issued or revoked, encryption key change. Each has a transaction id, a block time and a status. `enumerateContractActions` in `contract/src/wallet/capture.ts` in the passport repo is a reference. Amounts: a deposit shows its amount when its inbox entry opens. A send shows the amount from the journal of E07.03 (send to a wallet address), and "amount unknown" on a device that has no journal entry for it. Unfinished sends from the pending payments read of E07.03 appear as records in progress. The indexer read this story needs: the history of one account's contract actions, in order, with block times. The read belongs to the indexer seam, whose owner is E04.06 (indexer seam); this story names what it needs and does not add a second client. Paging, and a bound on how far back one call goes.

Out of scope: The screens, which are E08.02 (activity feed) and E08.04 (account home). Grant details, which are E10.07 (grant listing from chain). What a dApp may read, which is E17.07 (client read surface). Checking coins on chain, which is E07.01 for one coin and E07.04 (rebuild the coin store) for an account.

## Dependencies

**Blocked by:** E07.01 Inbox entries, the coin store, and deposits into an account; E04.06 Indexer seam: transaction status and account state. Soft: E07.03 (send to a wallet address) for the journal amounts and the pending payments read, with a stub until then.  
**Unlocks:** E08.02 Activity feed; E08.03 Send screens; E08.04 Account home: holdings and entry points; E08.05 Receive screen and deposit page

## Acceptance criteria

- On a local node, after a deploy and two deposits made through the builder of E07.01 (inbox, coin store and deposits), one unshielded and one shielded: holdings show both amounts, and the activity lists the three events in order with the right kinds.
- A subscriber sees holdings change after a deposit from another wallet lands, with no reload.
- Unit fixtures cover every record kind, including send out and the in-progress record, with a stub of the pending payments read. A test with a real send is added once E07.03 (send to a wallet address) exists.
- A fresh client with no journal lists the same events, with "amount unknown" where only the journal had the amount.
- With the indexer unreachable the reads give a typed error and mark the last value as stale. They never report empty.
